# Opening themes -- do these two bars contain a musical idea?

Five two-bar openings, taken from the five pieces you rated Best or
Good in the corrected baseline. Each is about two to three seconds.

A piece can turn good after a weak opening, so being cut from a good
piece does not make an opening a strong theme. That is what this
asks.

For each: **is there a musical idea here?** Write `YES`, `WEAK`, or
`NO`.

`YES` -- a figure you could recognise again if it came back.
`WEAK` -- something is there but it is not distinctive.
`NO` -- just notes starting.
