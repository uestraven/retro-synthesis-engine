carries the music Fermata actually composed: the arranger no longer
rescales the timeline by tempo, and a dropped opening bar no longer
shifts a piece a bar late.

Rate each piece **Best / Good / OK / Bad**, judged on its own rather
than against the others. Brief comments optional and welcome --
melody, direction, pacing, how it ends.

The question is absolute: how much of this would you use in a game?

All files are peak-normalised to one level, and filenames carry no
information about scenario or seed.
