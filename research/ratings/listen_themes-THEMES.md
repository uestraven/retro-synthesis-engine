# Opening themes -- do these 4 bars establish an idea?

Five 4-bar openings from the five pieces you rated
Best or Good in the corrected baseline.

**The question is whether a PITCHED idea gets established** -- a
melody, a bass riff, any pitched figure. Drums alone do not count:
this experiment is about melodic development, and a busy drum part
can make an opening sound like it has content when it has no tune in
it. That is what happened at two bars.

Each theme has two files. Play `theme_NN.wav` first; if the drums
make it hard to tell, play `theme_NN.pitched.wav`, which is the same
music with the drum channel muted.

For each: **is there a musical idea here?** Write `YES`, `WEAK`, or
`NO`.

`YES` -- a pitched figure you could recognise again if it came back.
`WEAK` -- something pitched is there but it is not distinctive.
`NO` -- no pitched idea. Drums only, or just notes starting.
