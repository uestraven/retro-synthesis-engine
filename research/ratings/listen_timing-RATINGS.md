# Timing correction -- A/B listening

Twelve pairs. Each pair is **one composition, arranged two ways**.
The model, the seed, the notes, the channel assignment and the
hardware repair are identical within a pair. Only the conversion
from the composer's timeline to the NES timeline differs.

For each pair, play `A` then `B` and record **A**, **B**, or **=**
for no meaningful preference. Comments on pacing, melody, rhythm
and how the piece ends are the most useful part.

Two things stated up front rather than hidden:

- **You will often hear which is which.** One version of a slow
  piece can last three times as long as the other. This test asks
  which you prefer, not whether you can tell them apart.
- **A and B are shuffled independently per pair.** Working out one
  pair tells you nothing about the next.

Both files in a pair are peak-normalised to the same level, so
loudness carries no information.
