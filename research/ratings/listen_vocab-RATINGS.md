# Harmonic vocabulary -- 24 matched pairs

Each pair is **one composition with two chord vocabularies.** Same
style, seed, key, tempo, motif and chord-change rate. Only the
progressions differ. A/B is shuffled and exactly balanced.

The melody has to change where a new chord requires different
pitches -- that is expected, not a fault.

Three answers per pair:

1. **Would A pass as NES game music?** yes / no
2. **Would B pass?** yes / no
3. **Which is better?** `A`, `B`, or `=`

Comments matter most here. Worth noting: whether the harmony is more
interesting, whether tension resolves satisfyingly, and anything
that sounds like a wrong note.
