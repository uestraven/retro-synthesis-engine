# View 2 -- whole pieces, does the later material develop the opening?

30 pieces, eight bars each. Same pieces as view 1, whole.

**Do this view after view 1.** It needs the opening, which view 1
deliberately withholds.

One question: **does the second half relate musically to the first?**
Write `STRONG`, `PARTIAL`, or `ABSENT`.

`STRONG` -- the opening idea comes back, answered, varied or
developed. It does not need to be repeated exactly; a figure that
returns transposed or with changed rhythm counts fully.
`PARTIAL` -- some connection, or the same texture without the idea.
`ABSENT` -- the second half could belong to a different piece.

A piece that simply copies its opening bars verbatim is `PARTIAL`,
not `STRONG` -- copying is not development.
