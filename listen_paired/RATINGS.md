# Paired length test -- ratings

Rate every take on the same 1-6 scale you used before, where **1 is
the best and 6 is the worst**. Judge each take on its own; do not try
to work out which takes belong together.

One thing this test cannot hide: half the takes are twice as long as
the other half, so the length of a file does tell you which arm it
is in. What stays hidden is the scenario, the seed, and which long
take extends which short one. Rate what you hear; a longer piece
that goes nowhere should score worse than a short one that works.
